# References Directory

This directory holds literature PDFs, bibliography sources, and geometric/theory
working notes. A file being present here does not mean its claims have been
integrated into the canonical methodology.

- Keep source papers and bibliographies here.
- Keep derivations clearly labeled as proved, conjectured, or empirical.
- Link a theoretical claim to its assumptions and to the experiment that tests
  the corresponding empirical prediction.
- Do not treat generated reports or exploratory calculations as primary
  literature.

The current scope deliberately excludes an equal-agility impossibility theorem;
historical geometric analysis is retained as background until its assumptions
and proof status are resolved.
