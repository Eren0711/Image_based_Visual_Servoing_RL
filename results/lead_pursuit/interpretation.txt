------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS / NOTES:
- Zero-shot transform: the policy was TRAINED on pure-pursuit observations,
  so feeding it a lead-shifted error is itself out-of-distribution. A drop
  in cruise success at larger T is expected for this reason and does not by
  itself prove lead pursuit is a bad idea --- only that this policy cannot
  exploit it without retraining.
- The lead uses the DKF filtered image velocity (obs[2:4]), not the raw
  noisy measurement; the DKF velocity is smoother but lags fast turns.
- Unit handling: position and velocity obs channels are normalized
  differently (tan(half_fov) vs MAX_DP=10.0); the wrapper converts to a
  common raw-p_bar unit before adding, so the lead is dimensionally correct.
- Success radius for reference: d_success = 2 m.
