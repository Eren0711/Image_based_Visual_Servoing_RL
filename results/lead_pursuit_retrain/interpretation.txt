LEAD-PURSUIT RETRAIN — INTERPRETATION
============================================================

Warm-started from HardNet-D locked (lambda=0.05); LeadPursuitWrapper T=0.3
active during BOTH training and evaluation. Trained against the 6-DOF evader with
maneuver distribution cruise/turn/weave/break/random = 20/40/15/15/10%. All PPO
hyperparameters inherited from the checkpoint (HardNetDPPO.load). Perception stack =
noise+delay+DKF+CBF-context (matches the agility & zero-shot eval distribution).

Final eval (100 ep/mode, seeds 0-99, deterministic, T=0.3):
  Cruise=15%  Turn=0%  Weave=1%  Break=0%  Random=0%  mean=3.2%

1) Steady-turn success: 0%  -> above 0%? NO; above 10%? NO; above 30%? NO
2) Training step where steady-turn first appeared: never (stayed 0%)
3) Cruise vs baseline: retrained=15% vs pure-pursuit=71% -> hurt
4) Best overall mean success: retrained=3.2% (pure-pursuit=24.2%, zero-shot=11.2%)
5) FOV retention (retrained): Cruise=94%, Turn=92%, Weave=93%, Break=94%, Random=94%

OVERALL CONCLUSION: LEAD PURSUIT FUNDAMENTALLY INSUFFICIENT.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP: Proceed to a temporal-memory architecture (recurrent/LSTM policy or stacked observation history). A single-step linear lead, even when trained on, cannot capture a sustained curving target; and consistent with the agility ablation and Claim B, the fixed-camera + equal-agility geometry is the binding limit.
